Finger-pair sweep

Both IDTs set to the same Np, single acoustic transit, no tuning. Two apertures: the current W, and the W that gives |Z| = Z₀ for each Np (lowest loss without tuning). Dotted line: current Np; dashed line: Npopt = √(π/4K²).

Model and limits

  • IDT: impulse (crossed-field) model, uniform single-electrode IDT, metallization ratio 0.5. Ga = 8K²CsWf₀Np²(sin x/x)², Ba its Hilbert transform, Ct = NpCsW. No electrode reflections, mass loading, diffraction or bulk-wave loss.
  • Delay line: two IDTs with the same aperture and period; acoustic transfer √(Ga₁Ga₂)·sinc·sinc·e−jωτ. With regeneration on, the exact Y→S conversion includes the triple-transit echo.
  • Ports: probe (Z₀) → optional tuning L → pad (shunt Cpad) → lead (R, L) → finger resistance (2/3)ρW/(a h Np) → IDT. Feedthrough Cft couples the two pad nodes.
  • Substrate: v and K² for Y-Z and 128°Y-X from Datta (1986), as tabulated by Kawada et al., arXiv:2504.12752 (2025). Cs is an estimate, Cs = ε₀ + √(ε′₁₁ε′₃₃ − ε′₁₃²) with clamped LiNbO₃ permittivity (Kushibiki et al. 1999). X-, Y- and Z-cut at a free angle get Cs only; enter v and K² yourself until the partial-wave solver (Phase 2).
  • Pads: Cpad from a coplanar-waveguide estimate; replace it with a value from an open-pad dummy if you have one.
